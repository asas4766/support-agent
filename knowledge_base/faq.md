# NimbusCart Customer Support Knowledge Base

This is a fictional e-commerce company's FAQ, used as the agent's knowledge base.
Each "## " section below is treated as one retrievable chunk.

## Shipping times
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within Hong Kong and 7-14 business days
for international orders. Express shipping (available at checkout for an extra
fee) takes 1-2 business days domestically and 3-5 business days internationally.
Orders placed after 3 PM local time ship the following business day.

## Tracking an order
Once an order ships, a tracking link is emailed to the address on the account.
Customers can also see live tracking status by logging into "My Orders" on the
NimbusCart website or app. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to activate after
a shipping confirmation email is sent.

## Return policy
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided
they are unused and in original packaging. Final-sale items (marked clearly on
the product page) and gift cards are not eligible for return. Refunds are
issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of NimbusCart
receiving the returned item.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or color are free within 30 days of delivery.
Customers should initiate an exchange from the "My Orders" page; a prepaid
return label is generated automatically. The replacement item ships once the
original is scanned by the courier.

## Damaged or incorrect items
If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, customers should contact support
within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. NimbusCart
covers return shipping in these cases and ships a replacement at no cost,
or issues a full refund if the customer prefers.

## Payment methods
NimbusCart accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay.
Installment payments (3 or 6 months, interest-free) are available on orders
over HK$800 through supported card issuers, selectable at checkout.

## Order cancellation
Orders can be cancelled for free within 1 hour of placement from the "My
Orders" page. After that window, the order enters fulfillment and cannot be
cancelled directly — customers should contact support, who can attempt to
intercept the shipment or advise on the return process once it arrives.

## Account and password issues
Customers can reset their password from the login page using "Forgot
password," which sends a reset link valid for 30 minutes. If the linked email
is no longer accessible, support can verify identity using the order history
and update the account email manually.

## Loyalty program (NimbusPoints)
Customers earn 1 NimbusPoint per HK$10 spent. Points can be redeemed at
checkout in blocks of 100 points = HK$10 off. Points expire 12 months after
they are earned if unused. Loyalty status tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum) are
based on trailing-12-month spend and unlock free express shipping and early
access to sales.

## Contacting a human agent
This assistant can resolve most order, shipping, return, and account
questions directly. For anything it cannot resolve — fraud concerns, legal
requests, or complex multi-order disputes — it will create a support ticket
so a human agent can follow up, typically within 1 business day.
